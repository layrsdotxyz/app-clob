# NORMAL fill postings

This story makes the existing native NORMAL-match economics an explicit,
validated enclave-ledger transaction. It does not change matching, fee policy,
MINT/MERGE, resolution, custody contracts or public projections.

## Private accounting model

For each NORMAL fill, the enclave posts two independently conserved assets.

Settlement cash (`USDC` in `base-usdc` or `ZEN` in `horizen-zen`):

```text
DEBIT  buyer UserOrderHold       seller proceeds + taker fee
CREDIT seller UserAvailable      seller proceeds
CREDIT Layrs FeeRevenue          taker fee
```

The outcome claim (`CLAIM:<market>:<outcome>`):

```text
DEBIT  seller UserOrderHold      filled quantity
CREDIT buyer UserPosition        filled quantity
```

The market collateral account is unchanged by a NORMAL transfer because the
claim already exists and remains backed by the same collateral. Creating or
burning claims is exclusively the later MINT/MERGE story.

The taker direction determines who economically bears the fee:

- incoming BUY: the buyer hold pays notional plus fee and the seller receives
  full notional;
- incoming SELL: the resting buyer hold pays notional, the seller receives
  notional less fee, and the remainder is fee revenue.

In both cases `cash debit = seller proceeds + fee`. Fee schedule versioning,
rebates and referrals remain separate from this story; this transition accepts
only the deterministic fee amount calculated by the existing fee engine.

## Atomicity and replay

All fills generated by one incoming order, its initial reserve and any
remainder release are applied to a cloned ledger and committed once. Validation
consumes the exact cash, fee and claim transfers described by each fill. Any
missing, duplicated or mismatched leg rejects the whole command without a
state-root or sequence change.

Replay protection includes an enclave-derived key for every deterministic fill
ID. Changing the operator request/idempotency key therefore cannot repost a
committed fill. These replay keys survive encrypted snapshot serialization.
The existing processed-command archive lets the API recover the signed receipt
after a commit-success/response-loss event without executing the command again.

## Privacy boundary

`NormalFillPosting`, owners, exact balances and ledger legs remain inside the
attested enclave and encrypted journal. Public audit artifacts retain only the
existing one-time pseudonyms and aggregate fill economics. No wallet, email,
opaque owner, order row, private position, hold or posting is added to backend
or public APIs.

## Certification coverage

- partial and full fills;
- BUY-taker and SELL-taker fee directions;
- exact buyer/seller positions, available balances, holds, fees and unchanged
  market collateral;
- 10,000 generated cash/fee/quantity combinations;
- one-atomic-unit cash/claim dust;
- duplicate fill IDs in one batch;
- same fill evidence under a different operator key;
- mismatched fees and malformed account ownership;
- snapshot recovery and lost-response retry without a second mutation.

Funded deployment reconciliation remains a controlled release gate and is not
performed by this implementation story.
